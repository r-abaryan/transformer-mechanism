## What we will stop doing

No further head/source/row patches, normalization budget extensions, or attempts to decode every activation in this cycle. Preserve negative results and their limits. Reopen only for a demonstrated implementation defect or a new decision that requires the answer.

## What counts as progress now?

One controlled change must improve a predefined held-out outcome while preserving the original task. An unsuccessful comparison is a valid completed result. One result document, one audit, one keep/reject decision. A threshold miss does not trigger tuning, new seeds or longer training.

The positional, repeated-key, activation, width and query-exposure comparisons are executed and closed. Historical scores remain reference points, not interchangeable baselines.

[KV-cache equivalence](../topics/attention/kv-cache-results.md) is executed and closed: all 120 cases and the independent audit pass. No further experiment is selected. The frozen decision records the pre-execution plan.

[Shared-KV arithmetic](../shared-kv-results.md) is also executed and closed. Further quality/resource comparison requires its own matched training decision.

[MHA/MQA training](../mqa-training-results.md) is executed and closed. MQA passes the fixed resource/quality tolerances; no further experiment is selected.

[MQA GPU cached decoding](../topics/attention/mqa-decoding-results.md) is executed and closed. Correctness and cache savings pass; repeatable speedup fails. No automatic profiling or timing sweep is selected.

[Cache precision](../topics/attention/cache-precision-results.md) is executed and closed. FP32 preserves final predictions on this subset; FP16/INT8 fail the strict zero-change rule. No automatic quantizer sweep is selected.
