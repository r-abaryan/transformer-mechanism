# Reciprocal cross-attention output transfer

Frozen scope, 2026-10-08. Does the successful combined-exposure model's complete projected cross-attention output restore copying and stopping inside the weak same-seed model?

- No training. Use seed 3767, update 6,000, cross-only and combined checkpoints from `copy_learning_trajectory`, verified against its persisted audit. Reuse ordinary pools from `copy_position_sites/data.json`: train/validation/test lengths 3–6 and test lengths 8/10. These are opened diagnostic pools, not fresh confirmation. No layout pools or stage search.
- Two recipients × four modes: native, self-transfer, opposite-model transfer, opposite-model magnitude only. Both donor and recipient receive the identical source and recipient prefix. Replace both heads' projected cross output, including projection bias, before decoder norm2. Keep recipient residual, normalization, FFN and classifier. Apply at every step, including terminal decisions, without checking the true length to select an intervention.
- Magnitude control: multiply the native cross vector by donor norm/native norm, separately per batch row and decoder position. Native norms at most 1e-12 remain unchanged and are counted; any such rows limit the control. No learned coordinate alignment.
- CPU float64, one thread, chunks of 64. Teacher forcing uses BOS plus source content; labels are content plus EOS. Generation begins with BOS, stops at first EOS, and has the unchanged length+3 safety limit. No oracle content, forced EOS or EOS suppression. Record exact outputs and teacher logits.
- Report every pool and all/distinct/repeated groups: full generated copy, generated content, teacher token/content accuracy and CE, early/on-time/late/missing EOS. Explicit null for absent groups. Report cross norms and norm ratios for teacher rows and active generated steps separately.
- Primary conditional-sufficiency gate: combined-to-weak transfer reaches at least 95% complete copying and on-time EOS in each long group, gains at least 10 percentage points in length-10 full copying, and every short train/validation/test group retains at least 99% complete copying. Native and self must reproduce saved results. Reciprocal sensitivity: weak-to-combined loses at least 10 points at length 10 while each short group remains at least 99%. Magnitude specificity: full transfer beats magnitude-only by at least 10 points at length 10, conditional on the primary gate. Report each gate separately. Failed gates trigger no tuning.
- Before scoring: saved initialized models through actual evaluator and separate reference audit; unequal source/prefix lengths, terminal rows, absent groups, supplied perfect and wrong logits, self identity, and a deliberately corrupted saved output that the auditor rejects. Native functions use hooks; reference explicitly reconstructs matrix rotations, attention, layer norms, FFN and scalar decoding. Verify all real rows against reference and all native saved outputs against original final scores. Tolerances: logits 1e-9, metrics 1e-9; generated tokens exact. Verify dependencies before and after.

Interpretation: package sufficiency is conditional on recipient coordinates and residual. Reciprocal short-task damage makes transplant incompatibility a live explanation. Even a successful transfer does not establish which training updates created the signal or that it uniquely mediates exposure.

Equation inspected: multi-head output and residual normalization in [Vaswani et al., §§3.1–3.2](https://arxiv.org/html/1706.03762v7#S3). Replacement, controls and gates above are local experimental choices; the paper does not predict these scores.
