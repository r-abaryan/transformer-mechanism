# Fixed-memory readout-scale control

Frozen before intervention evaluation, 2026-10-04. Question: how much held-out CE deterioration is removable by restoring the earlier classifier scale while keeping final memory and class rankings fixed? The endpoint results are already opened; this is a diagnostic intervention, not fresh confirmation or recipe selection. No training, optimizer update, label-based scale selection or temperature sweep.

For classifier z=Wr+b, form A=[W|b] and center its six class rows: C=A-mean_classes(A). Derive exactly one positive scale per seed, s=||C_3000||_F/||C_12000||_F. Reject nonfinite/zero norms; do not clip or reverse a scale if it fails to help. Replace final A with mean_classes(A_final)+s*C_final. Thus z_control=mean_classes(z_final)+s*(z_final-mean_classes(z_final)). Positive s preserves class ordering; bias must be scaled too. The augmented norm treats the intercept as an input coordinate of one and is a local, coordinate-dependent definition, not a unique measure of prediction sharpness.

Use seeds 3461/3463 and the same full train/validation/test pools at loads 2/8/16/32 as the audited step-3000/12000 exhaustive-feedback endpoints. Keep final key/value/beta tensors exact. Preserve every historical artifact. Write separate controlled evaluation checkpoints (model only, no resumable optimizer), logits, report and audit. CPU float64, one thread. Actual controlled-model forward evaluation covers all pools; independent matrix-equation audit reloads each controlled checkpoint. Original endpoint forward audits are reused by their bound hashes. Verify fixed-memory states on a two-history fixture per cell and exact non-head state equality.

Report every seed, split, load and all/unique/repeat group: count, accuracy, complete maps, old/final/control CE, loss increases/decreases and prediction changes. Require exactly zero changed winners and identical accuracy/maps; numerical metrics tolerance 1e-9, comparisons epsilon 1e-12. Fail visibly if finite-precision ordering changes. Keep the four historical correct_both/wrong_both/rescued/newly_wrong cohorts fixed by the original endpoints. Report each cohort's final/control CE, true probability and winner confidence, and signed contribution N^-1 sum(CE_final-CE_control). Empty group means are null and contributions zero. Correct predictions may incur higher CE; report these costs too.

For old-to-final positive excess >1e-12, report (CE_final-CE_control)/(CE_final-CE_old); otherwise null. This signed descriptive recovery ratio can be negative or above one. It is not a fraction of a historical causal effect. Removing extra loss does not recover wrong answers or prove why training increased scale. No calibration/ECE conclusion, readout-direction replacement, memory swap or additional training is authorized by this diagnostic.

Scope/counts: 2 seeds x 3 splits x 4 loads =24 controlled cells; 2*(768+192+384)*(2+8+16+32)=155904 answers. Historical cohorts produce 24*3*4=288 CSV rows, with overlapping group summaries. These are two reused development seeds, not 24 independent experiments. Compare held-out load 32 and show all train/validation/test loads without assumed benefit.

Before formal evaluation, exercise the actual evaluator and independent audit on an initialized saved model plus supplied logits, absent groups and ties. Check common-class head-offset invariance, finite positive norms, exact unchanged memory, cohort reconciliation and intentional wrong scale/non-head/metric/label controls. Freeze passing preflight, protocol, producer, auditor and tests before loading trained scale values; persist derived scales before controlled evaluation. Stop after this one intervention, independent audit and short educational report.

Source inspected: [Guo et al. 2017, section 4.2, equation 9](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/guo17a/guo17a.pdf), positive-temperature softmax scaling and unchanged argmax. They select temperature using validation NLL; we use the weight-derived s with T=1/s and do not fit calibration. Class centering, augmented-norm matching and the fixed-cohort accounting are local derivations. The paper does not predict our scores.
