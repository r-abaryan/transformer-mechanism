Let A=[W|b], C=A-mean_classes(A). Hold memory and read vectors fixed, and vary only A(lambda)=mean_classes(A)+exp(lambda)*C. At lambda=0, for scores z and true class y, g=d CE/d lambda=sum_c p_c*(z_c-z_y). This centered-gap form avoids subtracting two large logits. Negative g means increasing scale locally lowers CE under scalar gradient descent; positive g means reducing it locally lowers CE. Correct answers have g<=0 mathematically; wrong answers need not have positive g. Scale does not repair ranking. These are local derivatives, not measured Adam updates or causal attribution of historical growth.
